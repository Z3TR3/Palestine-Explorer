Palestinian Governorates

Quds

Quds is one of the oldest cities in the world and has great historical and cultural importance.

Al-Khalil

Al-Khalil is an ancient Palestinian city famous for its history, traditional markets and architecture.

Yafa

Yafa is a historic coastal city famous for its sea, port and beautiful old city.

Khan Younis

Khan Younis is located in southern Gaza and is known for its history and agricultural areas.

Rafah

Rafah is located in the southern part of the Gaza Strip and has an important geographical location.

Gaza

Gaza is an ancient Mediterranean city with a long history, rich culture and strong traditions.
